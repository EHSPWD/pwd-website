dragon ball super is an anime based on two character's named goku and vegeta. these two are saiyans from another planet named planet namek. goku and vegeta are sent to earth whese they face many strong opponents, even gods and angels to train and get stronger.

goku is a simple minded person with the iq of a kid at most times, but when it comes to battle, his iq is one of the best in the anime.

while goku is simple minded, vegeta's mindset is all about his pride in the saiyan race. every battle vegeta has fought in, he's talked about either his or his opponents pride in their race. unlike goku, vegeta likes to transform into his strongest form at the start of the battle while goku uses almost all of his forms to see how strong his opponents are and what transformation he needs to beat them.
