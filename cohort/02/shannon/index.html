What is the neigbourhood?

The neighbourhood is a american alternative rock band, formed in Newbury Park, California 2011.They are known for dark pop and single hit "sweater weather", The band consist of vocalist Jesse Rutherford, guitarists Jeremy Freedman and Zach Abels bassist Mikey Margott and drummer Brandon Fried.

2. (CAS) Cigarettes After Sex

What is Cigarettes After Sex?

CAS is an American dream pop band formed in El paso, Texas by Greg Gonzalez in 2008. The band is known for ethereal/dream-like musical style the lyrics based on the themes of love and romance.

This is another band that is my favorite, because it sets a feeling of calmness and an estatic feeling.

3. Tv Girl

What is Tv Girl?

Tv Girl is an American indie pop band formed in San Diego, California 2010. The band is known for its dreamy and lo-fi sound.
