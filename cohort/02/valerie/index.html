The best food in Venezuela

The best Venezuelan food is distinguished by its versatility and intense flavors, with the arepa (stuffed corn bread) being the most emblematic dish of each day, followed by the pabellón criollo (shredded meat, rice, black beans and plantains) as the national dish.
