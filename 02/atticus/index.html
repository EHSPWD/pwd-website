About Us

Immigrant Protection & Safety is a non-profit organization founded over 10 years ago that is dedicated to provide immigrant communities in danger of deportation or detainment by federal law enforcement with essential support during the current Project 2025.

Our Mission

The main goal of Immigrant Protection & Safety is to make sure that both documented and undocumented immigrants have the necessary resources and the same opportunities as other citizens in order to defend themselves from the harsh reality of ICE incidents and involvement.

Handling Interactions with ICE

It is extremely important to be informed of your rights as a citizen when encountering ICE agents and officials at your door or on the street. The key points to handling a conversation with ICE are as follows:

  • DO NOT OPEN THE DOOR
  • DO NOT ANSWER ANY QUESTIONS ASKED BY ICE
  • DO NOT SIGN ANY LEGAL DOCUMENTS
  • USE YOUR CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS
